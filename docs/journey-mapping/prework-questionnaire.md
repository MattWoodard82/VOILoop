# VOILoop Customer Journey Mapping Pre-Work Questionnaire

## Purpose
Please complete this before the journey mapping workshop. Your responses will be used to build evidence-based journeys for four personas:
- Participant
- Wellness Director
- Admin
- Exec

It is best practice to explicitly avoid assumptions and feature-led storytelling. Please provide concrete examples, artifacts, or references where possible.

## How to Answer
- Prefer specific examples over general statements.
- If unsure, write “Unknown” (do not guess).
- Add links or references to evidence (screenshots, analytics, support notes, code paths, issue links).

---

## Section A — Business and Product Context (Global)
1. What business outcomes must this product improve in the next 2 quarters?
2. What are the top 3 user outcomes we believe matter most?
3. What constraints are fixed for now (budget, staffing, compliance, timelines, technology)?
4. Which current UX problems are most harmful to demos? Which are most harmful in real use?
5. What would make this workshop a failure? What would make it a success?

## Section B — Persona Profile: Participant

### 1) Persona Definition
1. Who is this participant segment (demographic/behavioral traits that matter to product use)?
2. What context are they in when using VOILoop (time, place, device, urgency)?
3. What are their primary motivations and desired outcomes?

### 2) Jobs-to-be-Done
4. What is the functional job they are hiring the product to do?
5. What emotional or social jobs are relevant? (For example: feeling reassured, feeling in control, feeling seen, or feeling embarrassed if they cannot complete a task.)
6. What alternatives do they use today (including manual workarounds)?

### 3) Triggers and Entry Points
7. What event triggers them to start this journey?
8. Where do they enter first (channel/page/app flow)?
9. What expectation do they have before the first interaction?

### 4) Current Journey (As-Is)
10. List the actual steps they take today from trigger to outcome.
11. At each step, what do they do, think, and feel? (For example: frustrated because a task takes too many clicks, reassured by a clear confirmation, or anxious when unsure whether something saved.)
12. Where do they hesitate, abandon, or ask for help? (For example: stopping mid-flow because they're unsure what happens next, leaving to get help, or coming back later.)
13. What errors/confusions occur most often?

### 5) Touchpoints and Systems
14. Which interfaces/channels are touched (email, web app, mobile, SMS, staff handoff)?
15. What backend/system dependencies shape this journey? (For example: device integration)
16. Which steps rely on non-scalable human intervention?

### 6) Pain Points and Root Causes
17. What are the top 5 pain points by impact?
18. For each pain point, what do we believe is the root cause?
19. Which pain points are symptom-level vs systemic?

### 7) Measures and Signals
20. What signals indicate journey success/failure today?
21. Which metrics exist, and where do they come from?
22. What key telemetry is missing?

### 8) Risks and Assumptions
23. What assumptions are we making about this persona that may be wrong?
24. What accessibility, trust, or privacy concerns may exist?
25. What do we need to validate in the workshop?

## Section C — Persona Profile: Wellness Director

### 1) Persona Definition
1. Who is the Wellness Director user in our context (responsibilities, constraints, incentives)?
2. What outcomes are they accountable for?
3. What is their tolerance for workflow complexity?

### 2) Jobs-to-be-Done
4. What core job do they need VOILoop to do reliably?
5. What coordination/communication jobs are part of their workflow?
6. What do they do outside VOILoop to complete the same job today?

### 3) Triggers and Entry Points
7. What events initiate their interaction?
8. What cadence is typical (daily/weekly/monthly/ad hoc)?
9. What preconditions must be true before they can proceed?

### 4) Current Journey (As-Is)
10. List their end-to-end steps in real usage.
11. Which steps are high-friction or cognitively heavy? (For example: remembering prior context, interpreting a dense report, or coordinating multiple people at once.)
12. Where do they experience uncertainty or rework? (For example: unclear ownership, needing to correct data, or not knowing if the workflow progressed.)
13. What breaks when volume increases?

### 5) Touchpoints and Systems
14. Which tools/systems must they use in sequence?
15. Where are handoffs between people or teams?
16. Where do data quality issues show up?

### 6) Pain Points and Root Causes
17. Top 5 pain points and business/user impact.
18. Suspected root causes for each.
19. Which pain points are “quick wins” vs “structural redesign” candidates?

### 7) Measures and Signals
20. What KPIs or operational metrics matter most to this persona?
21. What reports/dashboards are used, and how trusted are they?
22. What metric blind spots are currently risky?

### 8) Risks and Assumptions
23. What assumptions might overfit to one organization/site?
24. What policy/compliance constraints are non-negotiable?
25. What unknowns should be explicitly parked for follow-up?

## Section D — Persona Profile: Admin

### 1) Persona Definition
1. What kinds of admins do we have (roles/permission levels)?
2. What does “successful administration” look like?
3. What failure modes cause downstream user pain?

### 2) Jobs-to-be-Done
4. What administrative jobs are most frequent?
5. Which jobs are most critical but infrequent?
6. What jobs are currently too error-prone?

### 3) Triggers and Entry Points
7. What events require admin action?
8. What queues/inboxes/reports trigger their work?
9. What expectations exist for site uptime? (For example: when will users be using this site?)

### 4) Current Journey (As-Is)
10. Describe the current workflow steps for top admin tasks.
11. Where is permissioning unclear or brittle?
12. Where do admins need tribal knowledge to proceed? (For example: knowing a hidden rule, understanding an exception path, or remembering which person to contact.)
13. Which tasks require backtracking across screens?

### 5) Touchpoints and Systems
14. What systems are involved (identity, billing, reporting, support tooling)?
15. Where do integrations fail or create delays?
16. What data fields are frequently incomplete/inaccurate?

### 6) Pain Points and Root Causes
17. What are the highest-impact admin pain points?
18. Why do these pain points happen?
19. Which ones directly impact Participant or Wellness Director experience?

### 7) Measures and Signals
20. Which admin efficiency and quality metrics matter?
21. What indicators show preventable errors?
22. Which admin activities are currently unmeasured?

### 8) Risks and Assumptions
23. What assumptions exist about admin skill/time availability?
24. What security/governance risks are top-of-mind?
25. What needs policy clarification before design changes?

## Section E — Persona Profile: Exec

### 1) Persona Definition
1. Which executive roles consume VOILoop outputs?
2. What decisions do they make using this product?
3. What outcomes define ROI from their perspective?

### 2) Jobs-to-be-Done
4. What strategic job is VOILoop supporting for execs?
5. What confidence/credibility needs exist around the data?
6. What alternatives do they use if VOILoop is insufficient?

### 3) Triggers and Entry Points
7. What events or reporting cycles trigger usage?
8. What is the “moment of truth” for this persona?
9. What pre-read do they need before engaging?

### 4) Current Journey (As-Is)
10. Outline current consumption/decision workflow.
11. Where is interpretation difficult? (For example: reading a dashboard without enough context, comparing trends across time, or understanding what a metric actually means.)
12. Where do delays reduce decision quality?
13. What causes distrust in outputs? (For example: stale data, missing source context, conflicting reports, or a number that doesn't match what the person expects.)

### 5) Touchpoints and Systems
14. Which dashboards/reports/channels are used?
15. Where do manual exports or slide-building steps occur?
16. Where are narrative/context gaps in reporting artifacts?

### 6) Pain Points and Root Causes
17. Top executive pain points and consequences.
18. Root cause hypotheses.
19. Which pain points damage adoption or renewal potential?

### 7) Measures and Signals
20. What north-star and supporting metrics matter to execs?
21. Which indicators are lagging vs leading?
22. What outcomes do we currently fail to measure?

### 8) Risks and Assumptions
23. What assumptions are we making about decision workflows?
24. What governance, privacy, or reputational risks matter most?
25. What must be clarified live in the workshop?

## Section F — Cross-Persona Alignment
1. Where do persona goals conflict?
2. Where does one persona’s “efficiency” create another persona’s friction?
3. Which journey steps are shared across all personas?
4. What system constraints repeatedly appear across personas?
5. Which opportunities seem highest value and lowest risk?
